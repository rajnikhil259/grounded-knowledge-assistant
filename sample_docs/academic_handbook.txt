1. ATTENDANCE
Every student must have at least 75% attendance in each course to be allowed to sit the end-semester examination.
Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation by submitting a valid medical certificate to the Dean of Academics. Condonation can be granted only once in an academic year.
Students with attendance below 65% are detained in that course and must repeat it in the next offering.

2. EVALUATION AND GRADING
Each course is evaluated as follows: mid-semester examination 30%, end-semester examination 50%, and continuous assessment (assignments and quizzes) 20%.
Grades are awarded on a 10-point scale: O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8, B+ = 7, B = 6, C = 5, P = 4, and F = 0. The minimum passing grade is P.
A student must have a minimum CGPA of 5.5 at the end of the programme to be awarded the degree.

3. RE-EVALUATION AND SUPPLEMENTARY EXAMS
A student may request re-evaluation of an end-semester answer script within 7 days of the result declaration. The fee is Rs. 500 per course.
Students who receive an F grade may appear for the supplementary examination, which is held in July every year. The maximum grade awarded in a supplementary examination is B.

4. LIBRARY RULES
Each student may borrow a maximum of 4 books at a time for a period of 14 days. Books can be renewed once if no other student has reserved them.
A late return attracts a fine of Rs. 5 per day per book. For a lost book, the student must pay the replacement cost of the book plus a processing charge of Rs. 200.

5. HOSTEL RULES
The hostel gate closes at 10:30 PM every night. Late entry requires written permission from the warden.
Visitors are allowed only in the visitor room and only until 6:00 PM. Room change requests must be submitted within the first 2 weeks of the semester.

6. ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
The first plagiarism offence results in a score of zero for that assessment. A second offence results in an F grade in the course. A third offence results in suspension from the institute for one semester.
All cases are reviewed by the Academic Integrity Committee, and the student has the right to present their case before the decision is final.

7. LEAVE
Medical leave must be supported by a medical certificate submitted within 5 days of returning to campus. Leave taken without approval is counted as absence for attendance purposes.

8. MERIT SCHOLARSHIPS
Students with a CGPA of 9.0 or above receive a 50% tuition fee waiver for the following year. Students with a CGPA from 8.5 to 8.99 receive a 25% tuition fee waiver.
The scholarship is reviewed every year and is withdrawn if the student's CGPA falls below the required level or if the student is found guilty of an academic integrity violation.
